<h1 align="center">
  Hello, I'm Jessica! 👋🏾
</h1>

I'm a PhD student in **Computational Biology** at the **University of Melbourne**. I'm a software engineer turned computational biologist, and I love building tools that help us understand biology and medicine.

**Here's a bit about me:**

- 🔬 **Now:** PhD in computational biology, working with transcriptomic data and reproducible workflows.
- 💼 **Before:** backend engineering on TurboMedics, an LLM-powered healthcare AI product at Turbham Technologies.
- 🎓 **Studied:** Computer Science at Ambrose Alli University, and software engineering at [AltSchool Africa](https://altschoolafrica.com/schools/engineering) (2023).
- 🌍 I've worked on collaborative software projects across several countries and institutions.
- ✍️ I write on [JessiWrites](https://medium.com/@jessicaovabor) on Medium.
- 👩‍💻 Pronouns: she/her.
- 🎲 Fun facts: I love meeting new people, taking on new challenges, and finding joy in the little things.

<h2 align="center">
  🤝 Open-source contributions
</h2>

- **nf-core/modules:** new [CoGAPS](https://bioconductor.org/packages/CoGAPS) module for Bayesian non-negative matrix factorisation, with tests across Docker, Singularity and conda (merged: [nf-core/modules#13443](https://github.com/nf-core/modules/pull/13443)).
- **Bioconda:** fixed the `bioconductor-cogaps` recipe so CoGAPS ships with OpenMP multithreading enabled for conda and container users, with a test to prevent regressions (merged: [bioconda/bioconda-recipes#70579](https://github.com/bioconda/bioconda-recipes/pull/70579)).
- **CoGAPS (Bioconductor):** reported that the released `configure` script still builds without OpenMP, with a tested fix ([FertigLab/CoGAPS#166](https://github.com/FertigLab/CoGAPS/issues/166)).
- **ShinyButchR:** fix for the interactive NMF Shiny app built on ButchR (PR open upstream).
